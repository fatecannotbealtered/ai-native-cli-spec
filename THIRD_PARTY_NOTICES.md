## Product license seed and dependencies

`template/common/LICENSE` is the MIT license seed for a newly instantiated
product, with copyright placeholders for that product's owner. The seed does
not override licenses of copied specification material, SDKs or dependencies.
Keep the Apache-2.0 SDK LICENSE files and source copyright/SPDX notices when
vendoring, and include their license texts in distributed products. Product
owners may choose their own license for original product code; keep README,
package metadata and Skill license fields consistent with that choice.

External dependencies retain their respective licenses. Dependency lockfiles,
upstream notices and historical tags are unchanged by this licensing update.
